description=Apollo MP Sync makes supported multiplayer actions clearer across clients in [b]Project Zomboid 42.20.2[/b] while the vanilla server remains authoritative: reading, sitting, rest/sleep, climbing, floor transitions, vehicle seats and timed-action progress.
description=Project Zomboid remains authoritative for movement, physics, zombies, anti-cheat, relay and damage. Apollo never directly corrects player coordinates or vehicle physics.
description=
description=[h2]◆ HOW IT WORKS[/h2]
description=Optional [b]Native Assist[/b] is server-only, exact-runtime gated and fail-closed. If verification fails, Lua and vanilla behavior continue normally.
description=For supported PvP and PvE hits it performs bounded validation from accepted server facts and recent history. It never applies damage or bypasses vanilla anti-cheat.
description=[list]
description=[*]rewind is capped at 150 ms, with a 200 ms internal safety ceiling;
description=[*]cross-floor, blocked-line-of-sight and unloaded-area hits are rejected;
description=[*]stale facts, high RTT, jitter, runtime, fingerprint or hook mismatch use strict-current or vanilla fallback.
description=[/list]
